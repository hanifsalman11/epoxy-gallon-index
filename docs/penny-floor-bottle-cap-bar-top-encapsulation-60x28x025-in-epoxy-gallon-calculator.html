Frequently Asked Questions (Penny Floor / Bottle Cap Bar Top Encapsulation (60x28x0.25 In))

How many gallons of epoxy resin do I need for Penny Floor / Bottle Cap Bar Top Encapsulation (60x28x0.25 In)?

Using the volumetric formula (Length × Width × Depth ÷ 231), 60.0" × 28.0" × 0.25" equals 1.82 net gallons. Adding a 10% buffer for seal coating and mixing-cup residue gives 2.0 US Gallons (8.0 Quarts or 256 fluid ounces).

What happens if I pour 1:1 Self-Leveling Table-Top Epoxy too thick in one layer?

Epoxy cures via an exothermic (heat-generating) chemical reaction. Exceeding the rated pour depth traps heat in the center of the mass, causing thermal runaway (>200°F), severe amber yellowing, shrinkage cracks, and wave distortions.

How much mica powder or liquid pigment should I add per gallon of epoxy?

For a translucent ghost-tint river effect, add 1 to 2 grams (1/2 teaspoon) of mica powder per gallon. For a fully opaque metallic swirl across 2.0 gallons, mix 15 to 25 grams (1 to 2 tablespoons) of mica powder per gallon into Part A before adding Part B.
